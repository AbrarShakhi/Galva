# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Galva does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. This policy explains what the app
reads and stores on your device, and why.

## The short version

- Galva has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporters or trackers.
- Everything Galva stores stays on your device.

## What Galva reads

Galva asks for permission to read the photos and videos on your device. On Android 14 and later you
can choose to allow access to only some of them. Galva uses this access to show your media and does
not read any other files.

## What Galva stores on your device

- **A media index:** for each photo and video, its name, folder, type, size, dimensions, length,
  dates and its system identifier. This lets Galva open quickly and search without rescanning.
- **Your favourites and albums:** which items you marked as favourites and which albums you made.
- **Your settings:** theme, colour, grid size and similar preferences.

None of this leaves your device through Galva. Uninstalling Galva deletes it.

## Secrets

Photos and videos you move into Secrets are encrypted on your device with a key that comes from
your passphrase. Galva never stores your passphrase or recovery phrase, and nobody, including the
developer, can open Secrets without them. Secrets is excluded from Android backups, and Galva
blocks screenshots and screen recording while Secrets is open.

## Android backups

If you have turned on device backup in Android settings, Android may include Galva's media index,
favourites, albums and settings in that backup. This backup is run by Android and your backup
provider, not by Galva. Secrets is never included.

## Sharing and deleting

When you share a photo, Galva hands it to the app you pick in the Android share sheet, and that app
handles it under its own privacy policy. When you delete a photo, Android asks you to confirm
first.

## Children

Galva does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with the app and appear on GitHub with an updated
date.
