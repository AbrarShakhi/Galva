# About Galva

Galva is a gallery for Android that keeps your photos and videos where they belong: on your device.
It has no account, no cloud and no ads, and it cannot reach the internet at all.

## What it does

- **Timeline:** every photo and video, grouped by the day it was taken.
- **Albums:** your device folders, a Favourites album, and albums you make yourself. Adding a photo
  to an album never copies or moves the file.
- **Search:** find photos and videos by file or folder name, with filters for photos, videos and
  favourites.
- **Viewer:** full-screen photos with pinch to zoom, and videos with playback controls.
- **Secrets:** an encrypted vault that only your passphrase can open.
