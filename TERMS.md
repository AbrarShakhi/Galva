## Secrets

Secrets is designed so that only you can open it. That also means:

- **Keep your passphrase and recovery phrase safe.** If you lose both, everything in Secrets is gone
  for good. Nobody can reset them or recover your files.
- **Uninstalling Galva or clearing its storage destroys Secrets.** Restore anything you want to keep
  first.
- **Moving a photo into Secrets deletes the original** from your gallery. Copies elsewhere, such as
  cloud backups or other apps, are not affected.
- **Deleting from Secrets is final.**

## Deleting media

Deleting a photo or video in Galva deletes it from your device after Android asks you to confirm.
Galva does not keep a recycle bin.
